# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects provide technical direction, ensure design quality, and help the team make sound implementation decisions that align with long-term maintainability and system goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define or validate architectural direction and technical standards
- Review design trade-offs and guide solution choices
- Identify technical dependencies, constraints, and integration risks
- Support developers during implementation and troubleshooting
- Ensure the solution remains scalable, secure, and maintainable

### Goals
- Deliver solutions that are technically sound and sustainable
- Reduce rework caused by weak design decisions
- Align delivery with platform, security, and operational needs

### Typical Communication
- Architecture discussions and design reviews
- Technical dependency planning with engineering leads
- Escalation of critical design or platform risks to PMs and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to guide implementation choices and technical trade-offs
- Partners with Product Managers to assess feasibility and sequencing of roadmap items
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing delivery risks, technical dependencies, and timeline impacts
- Coordinates with Security Reviewers and QA on risk, compliance, and testability considerations

---

## Release Managers / Delivery Coordinators

### Role Summary
Release Managers or Delivery Coordinators ensure that work is ready to ship, deployments are well coordinated, and stakeholders are informed about release readiness, risks, and outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and coordinate release windows and deployment readiness
- Validate that release criteria, checklists, and rollback plans are complete
- Coordinate with engineering, QA, and stakeholders on launch timing
- Communicate release status, risks, and known issues to the broader team
- Support post-release verification and stabilization activities

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and deployment disruption
- Improve predictability and readiness for production changes
- Maintain clear communication before, during, and after releases

### Typical Communication
- Release plans and readiness reviews
- Deployment checklists and stakeholder updates
- Incident and rollback coordination when needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to align release timing with milestones and communication plans
- Collaborates with Developers and QA to confirm readiness, smoke tests, and release criteria
- Advises Product Managers and stakeholders on deployment impact, risk, and launch sequencing
- Escalates critical deployment issues to the appropriate leadership and support channels

---

## Security Reviewers

### Role Summary
Security Reviewers help teams identify and reduce security and compliance risk throughout planning, design, and delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Assess applications, services, and workflows for security risks
- Review design choices, dependencies, and change impacts for vulnerabilities
- Provide guidance on secure coding, access controls, and data handling
- Participate in incident investigation and remediation planning for security issues
- Support compliance and governance requirements when applicable

### Goals
- Minimize security exposure and protect users and organizational data
- Embed security review early in the delivery lifecycle
- Improve trust, resilience, and regulatory readiness

### Typical Communication
- Security design and risk reviews
- Findings, remediation tracking, and exception discussions
- Escalation of critical vulnerabilities and compliance concerns

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Developers by reviewing risks during design and implementation
- Works with Technical Leads to validate secure architecture decisions
- Informs Project Managers and Product Managers of risk, time, and priority impacts
- Escalates high-risk issues to leadership and security stakeholders when necessary

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaisons

### Role Summary
Customer Success or Stakeholder Liaison roles connect delivery teams to user and stakeholder realities, translating feedback, adoption concerns, and business needs into actionable product and project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and share customer, user, and stakeholder feedback
- Help prioritize issues based on business impact and adoption needs
- Support communication about roadmap, releases, and status updates
- Identify risks related to user experience, adoption, training, or support readiness
- Connect delivery progress to customer outcomes and expectations

### Goals
- Improve customer and stakeholder alignment with project value
- Reduce friction between product delivery and adoption outcomes
- Ensure stakeholder voices are represented in planning and release decisions

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback sessions and stakeholder briefings
- Release readiness and adoption updates
- Cross-functional feedback loops on product outcomes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to clarify customer needs and validate priorities
- Provides context to Project Managers for stakeholder communications and milestone planning
- Shares feedback with Developers and QA to identify usability and acceptance concerns
- Helps Technical Leads and Delivery Coordinators understand user impact and support readiness needs

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads help teams define, measure, and interpret success metrics so decisions are based on evidence rather than intuition.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, instrumentation needs, and measurement plans
- Monitor product or project performance, adoption, and quality trends
- Support experimentation, validation, and reporting for milestone reviews
- Help connect delivery outcomes to business and customer impact
- Recommend adjustments based on data trends and changes in operational signals

### Goals
- Improve decision quality through measurable evidence
- Align delivery decisions with defined success metrics
- Detect early signals of risk, impact, or opportunity

### Typical Communication
- KPI dashboards, reporting, and milestone reviews
- Measurement plans and analysis summaries
- Data-informed recommendations for roadmap or release decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to define measurable outcomes and success criteria
- Provides reporting to Project Managers for milestone and risk review
- Supports Developers and QA by clarifying instrumentation and validation needs
- Helps leaders interpret the business and user impact of delivery choices

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These role definitions help teams clarify accountability, decision ownership, and cross-functional handoffs across planning, execution, release, and review activities.
